## Research notes
- rippled applies a transaction against the PARENT ledger's close time; each `ledger_accept` adds exactly one second once ledger time leads the clock, so pausing the closer lets a test land in a ledger whose parent closed at T-1, T or T+1 (`effCloseTime` = max(rounded clock, parent + 1)). Source: rippled `LedgerTiming.h`; https://github.com/XRPLF/rippled/blob/develop/src/xrpld/app/tx/detail/Escrow.cpp
- Transactions placed in one open ledger share the parent, but their order at close is canonical, not submission order; so each success case uses its own escrow.
- TrustSet: a line opened by an account without DefaultRipple has NoRipple on the other side; the opener's limit 0 needs `tfSetNoRipple` to delete it (I-2.6-c). Source: https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/fungible-tokens/rippling
- `submitReliable` is idempotent for the same blob (I-2.6-e).
- Sources are in `packages/xrpl/SOURCES.md` already (escrow, accountdelete, accountset); no new URLs beyond those cited in the test headers.
