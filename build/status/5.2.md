## Targets (`tools/fuzz/targets/`)
core: condition-fulfillment, mint-memo (decode + verifySignedContract), gate-memo (gateMemo + verifyGatePassedRecord), dg1, sod-lds (parseSod, parseLds, fixtures parseSod), attestation (verifyAttestation/verifyOnboarding; bare payloads are re-signed by the right lawyer so the rules behind the signature run; accepted payload must satisfy the contracts schema), hybrid-envelope (valid keys re-sign the body; accepted must carry the hybrid `sig_algs` and two signatures), checkpoint-note, checkpoint-0x06-line (ML-DSA-44 line from fuzz bytes; accepted only if it is the signed blob), frost-round-messages (parsePoint/parseScalar canonical, openEnrollmentMessage, readRound1 round trip, dkg rounds), frost-complaint (P5.2-03: also re-signed by the complainer after the change; an upheld complaint must carry exactly the real evidence), xwing-ciphertext (rejected unless the exact sealed bytes).
contracts: api-requests (every route input), ws-messages, dsse-schemas, frost-schemas, records-events. Accepted: JSON round trip gives the same value.
zk: zk-trust-record (P5.2-04: schema and `checkTrustRecord` must agree; production refuses Sepolia, `_MOCK`, dev mode; demo accepts them), zk-result (ZkVerification, checkZkResult, ZkVerifyRequest into replayZkVerifier: must refuse with `ZK_RESULT_MISSING`).

Inputs: random bytes, unchanged seeds, byte edits of seeds, and one or two JSON field edits (delete, junk, tweak, leaf from another seed, `__proto__`). Seeds: `packages/contracts/examples`, `packages/core/vectors` (crypto-conditions, pq checkpoint golden), `packages/fixtures/demo`, `packages/zk/fixtures/sets`, and signed fixtures built in `src/fixtures.ts`. Hang check: a run over 1 s fails (measured after the call; a true infinite loop is stopped by the CI job timeout).

## Crashes found (build/ISSUES.md)
1. `parseLds` throws untyped asn1js errors on malformed BER (3 variants).
2. `judgeComplaint` throws on a wrong-length KEM key or too few fields.
3. `checkTrustRecord` accepts unsafe integers and non-strings (via `String()`) that the schema refuses.
4. Low: `parseDg1` non-minimal BER length; DKG replay throws plain Error; no condition parser exists.
Inputs are in `tools/fuzz/corpus/<target>/`; `corpus/known.json` lists the ones that still crash, and the corpus test expects them to crash and fails with "looks fixed" when they stop (remove the entry then). Fuzz runs record known crashes without failing, so they cannot hide new ones.
