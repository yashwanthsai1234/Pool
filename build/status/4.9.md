## Built
- **`src/pq/keys.ts`, `pqKeys({role, dbName})`.** Two 32-byte seeds per deal and role (ML-DSA-65 and X-Wing), sealed with AES-GCM under a fresh non-extractable device key. This is share 1's scheme, with the AAD `legx/vault/v1|id|field`. The row `dealId:role:pq` is in the `enrollment` store. Functions:
  - `create`, `publicKeys`;
  - `half` (the `PqHalf` for 4.2/4.7; it decrypts, expands, signs and zeroes inside the call);
  - `hybridSigner(dealId, pairId)` (contracts `SignerPort` plus `signRaw` for the API context; key ids `<pair>#ed25519` and `<pair>#ml-dsa-65`, OD-5/21/31);
  - `open(dealId, 'share'|'backup', pkg, aad)` (X-Wing).
- **`src/pq/apiRequest.ts`.** `apiRequestMessage`, and `apiRequestSigner(signRaw, ledgerIndex)`, which is the web client's `signRequest`.
- **`src/pq/binding.ts`.** `bindingFields` (pinned keys, then seal, then offer checks), and `pqBindingSession` (`signWith`, `add`, `status`, and `finish(crossSign, submit)`, which verifies with core before submit and returns the contracts `PqBinding`). It also exports `PQ_BINDING_TEXT` (waiting, Key mismatch, progress and the not-audited help text).
- Tests are in `test/pq/{keys,zeroize,binding,apiRequest,spy,perf}.browser.test.ts`, `interop.test.ts` and `support.ts`.

## Research notes
- noble post-quantum 0.7.1 (README): `keygen(seed)` takes a 32-byte seed, and `{ context }` is at most 255 bytes. It is not audited and claims no constant time. https://github.com/paulmillr/noble-post-quantum
- Probe `build/tmp/4.9/probe.mjs`: the X-Wing secret key is the 32-byte seed (`getPublicKey(seed)` equals the keygen public key), and the ML-DSA-65 secret key is 4,032 B. Draft: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-connolly-cfrg-xwing-kem/
- `pqKeyHash` is core's `tupleHash("legx/pq-keyset/v1", ...)`, which is 1.16's DQ-1 (DESIGN wins over "SHA-256").
- All URLs are in `packages/vault/SOURCES.md`.
