## Research notes and evidence
- Standalone 3.4.1 with ci/xrpld.cfg: `feature` reports Credentials `enabled:false, vetoed:true`, and there is NO Amendments ledger object (`ledger_entry` entryNotFound). So the [features] stanza is invisible to RPC; the only truth is the engine. `assertAmendments` therefore probes Credentials with `simulate` of a CredentialCreate and fails on `temDISABLED` (non-mutating; verified: tecNO_TARGET with the stanza, temDISABLED without).
- Retired list: Escrow, MultiSign, CryptoConditions, DepositAuth, DepositPreauth, MultiSignReserve, ExpandedSignerList, DeletableAccounts, DisallowIncoming, TicketBatch (3.2.0 retirement, r5-xrpl.md).
- `ledger_accept` moves close_time by at least about 1 s per close (wall clock); about 700 closes/s. Time travel of days is impossible, so `advanceCloseTime` caps at 20000 ledgers and throws.
- online_delete=256 removes ledger 1 after 256 ledgers, so the genesis hash/shape check cannot be unconditional. Standalone rule: network_id 0, loopback/private host only, and either ledger 1 has the genesis shape (parent hash zero, close_time 0, 1e17 drops) or peers==0 and validation_quorum==0. Mainnet genesis hash could not be sourced (web search found none), so no hash constant is embedded.
- Guard: allow-list (standalone: loopback/RFC1918 only; testnet/devnet: *.rippletest.net only) plus a Mainnet denylist plus NETWORK=mainnet. xrpl.js `connect()` itself sends server_info; this happens only after host checks pass.
- Sources in `packages/xrpl/SOURCES.md`.
