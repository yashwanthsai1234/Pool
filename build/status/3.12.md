## Research notes
- RFC 4998 5.2: renewal time-stamps the old timestamp field: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4998
- AWS KMS Sign: EXTERNAL_MU needs exactly 64 bytes, RAW max 4096: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/kms/latest/APIReference/API_Sign.html
- C2SP tlog-checkpoint: 0x06 covers only the first three lines; never sign inconsistent checkpoints: https://c2sp.org/tlog-checkpoint

## Design questions
- DQ-3.12-1: `@legx/core/pq` and `pqCheckpoint` import noble, so servers load noble for verify, sign and keygen. The test pins this to exactly those two files and shows sign/keygen are never called. A strict "no noble in the graph" needs core to verify through `node:crypto` (it.todo).
- DQ-3.12-2: new tags: `legx/renewal-timestamp/v1` (digest sent to the authority), `legx/service-key-rotation/v1` (attestation type field).
- Renewal TSA signatures are classical RFC 3161 (no PQ authority exists yet), as DESIGN 8.4 says.
