## What held (no finding)
- **FROST:**
  - `SigningSession` refuses nonce reuse and message or list swaps.
  - The relay checks the pair, session and pinned key.
  - The 72-hour delay and cancel hold.
- **PQ:** a checkpoint with the 0x06 line stripped is refused (`PQ_LINE_MISSING`).
- **API auth (OD-5):**
  - The nonce is single use, unique across the table, and burned only after the signature verifies.
  - Stale (more than 20) and future (more than 2) ledger indexes are refused.
  - The raw `pathname+search` is signed, and path params override the body and query.
- **Vault:**
  - `postMessage` checks both origin and source.
  - Recovery codes are 128-bit, with PBKDF2 at 600k rounds.
  - Every vault signature goes through `messageAllowed`.
- **TSA:** the certificate chain binds the signer to the root.
