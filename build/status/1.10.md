## Design
- API: `verifyBarVerification`, `verifyBarDealLink`, `openBarCommitment` (plus `normalizeBarNumber`, `barNumberSha256`). Inputs are the exact stored envelope bytes; the verifier is injected as `BarTrust { verify, verifierKeyIds }`, which `verifyHybrid(env, registry, at)` fits directly. Both ed25519 and ML-DSA key ids must equal the verifier's, else `WRONG_SIGNER`.
- Extra codes beyond the table: `ENVELOPE_INVALID`, `MALFORMED`, `ROLE_MISMATCH`, `TIME_INVALID` (future or non-integer ledger time), `BAR_NUMBER_MISMATCH` (verifier side, optional `opening`).
- Age: caller passes close times (Ripple seconds); `now - checkedAt > 2,592,000` is `STALE`.
- Bar number is validated against `[A-Za-z0-9-]{1,32}` before uppercasing (`ı` would uppercase to `I`).
