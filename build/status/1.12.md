## API
- `checkRelease(i: ReleaseInput): Finding[]`
  - It runs one rule per DESIGN 6.17 code, in table order. Every finding is `level: 'block'`, and `detail` holds the first failure (`<part>:<code>` for the per-side and per-slot rules).
  - A rule that throws becomes `INPUT_MALFORMED`. The gate never throws.
- `ReleaseInput`:
  - `profile`;
  - `ledger`: `{ index, hash, closeTime, account, objects, mintTx, credentials, opsAccounts, history }`, from ONE validated ledger. `history` holds the validated headers that records name.
  - `records`: the exact stored bytes of the passport, onboarding and bar attestations, the zk proof sets, and the `ops.bound`, `pq.bound` and `frost.dkg.completed` event envelopes, plus the enrollment key rows and `slotHolders` (three `{ ed25519, mlDsa65 }` key IDs per slot, in role order);
  - `deal`: `{ id, data: PublicDealData, mintTxHash, nftokenId, credentialSubjects, escrow, allowedTicketSequences? }`;
  - `submitter`;
  - `config`: `{ verifierIssuer, verifierKeyIds, attest? }`.
- `buildGatePassedRecord(input, eventFields, lawyerB: SignerPort, signSlotLb)`
  - It reruns the gate and throws `GATE_BLOCKED` unless every finding passes.
  - It builds the `release.gate.passed` in-toto event: the gate ledger, the findings, `relied_on` (name and SHA-256 per record) and `slot_lb_sig`.
  - `signSlotLb(envelopeForAllowList, msg)` must return slot LB's FROST signature. The record refuses a signature that does not verify strictly under `lawyerPubB`.
  - It then hybrid-signs the event with lawyer B and returns `{ envelopeBytes, leafHash = SHA-256(envelopeBytes) }`.
- `verifyGatePassedRecord(bytes, { deal, keys, lawyerB, at })` checks:
  - the hybrid envelope, by a lawyer B key bound to `pqKeyHash_LB` (OD-21);
  - the `LegxEvent` schema (a passed gate record);
  - the deal and the seal;
  - the slot LB signature.
- `gateMemo(leafHash)` returns `{ MemoType: hex("legx/release-gate/v1"), MemoData }` in uppercase (D22).

## How each code reads its input
- **LOCK_OK** calls `checkLock` with the sealed releaser and `minCancelAfter = closeTime + 86400`. A wrong submitter trips `RELEASER_SEALED` only.
- **CONTRACT_SEAL_OK** checks, in this order:
  - `validated`;
  - the stored hash equals `mintTx.hash`, which equals `hashes.hashSignedTx(tx_blob)`. So the memo and URI come from the signed transaction, never from a caller.
  - the transaction is applied, at or before the gate ledger;
  - it is an `NFTokenMint` by lawyerOpsA;
  - the meta `nftoken_id` equals the stored ID;
  - `parseNFTokenID` gives the issuer lawyerOpsA, and the taxon and flags equal the transaction's;
  - exactly one mint memo;
  - `verifySignedContract`.
- **PASSPORT_x / ONBOARDING** use 1.9's `verifyAttestation` and `verifyOnboarding`.
  - `sealedKeys.lawyerKeyIds` lists only that lawyer slot's holder keys, and only if their three ML-DSA keys hash to the sealed `pqKeyHash_L*` (OD-21).
  - `now` is the gate's close time.
- **BAR_x** reads `checked_at_ledger` from the verification bytes, looks it up in history and checks its hash. Then it calls `verifyBarDealLink` with a hybrid verifier and the sealed terms.
- **ATTORNEY_CREDENTIAL_x** filters in stages (type and subject, issuer, `lsfAccepted`, expiration) and reports the stage where the last candidate dropped.
- **OPS_BINDINGS_OK** calls `verifyOpsBinding`. Every term comes from the seal; the record supplies only `ops_pub` and the two signatures.
- **PQ_BINDINGS_OK** calls `verifyPqBinding` with the sealed seal, slot key and pqKeyHash. The cross-signature check is strict Ed25519.
- **SLOT_SETUP_OK** checks, in this order:
  - the group key is the sealed slot key;
  - `slotPolicy(roles)` equals both the sealed and the recorded policy;
  - each holder's enrollment key signed the `dkg-echo` FrostMsg over the transcript hash;
  - the three pair test signatures verify under the slot key (`testSignMessage`).
- **Event envelopes** (`ops.bound`, `pq.bound`, `frost.dkg.completed`): the gate checks the kind, the deal ID and the subject root, but not the wrapper's signature. Each payload is self-authenticating, since its signatures are over sealed values.

## Research notes
- **NFTokenID layout:** 2 bytes of flags, 2 of transfer fee, a 20-byte issuer AccountID, a 4-byte scrambled taxon and a 4-byte sequence, big-endian. https://xrpl.org/docs/references/protocol/data-types/nftoken
  - xrpl.js 5.3.0 `parseNFTokenID` (read in `node_modules/xrpl/src/utils/parseNFTokenID.ts`) decodes the issuer with `encodeAccountID` and unscrambles the taxon with the XLS-20 LCG (384160001, 2459).
- **The `tx` method:** `validated` true means final; `tx_blob` is returned in binary mode. https://xrpl.org/docs/references/http-websocket-apis/public-api-methods/transaction-methods/tx
  - `hashes.hashSignedTx` is sha512Half(`TXN\0` prefix + blob), read in `src/utils/hashes/hashLedger.ts`.
- **Credentials:** `lsfAccepted` is 0x00010000, and the expiry is compared with the close time. https://xrpl.org/docs/references/protocol/ledger-data/ledger-entry-types/credential
- **Memos:** at most 1 KB. https://xrpl.org/docs/references/protocol/transactions/common-fields
- All of these are listed in `packages/core/SOURCES.md`.

## Design questions
- **DQ-1.12-1, what slot LB signs.** `ReleaseGateRecord` carries `slot_lb_sig` inside the record, so the FROST signature cannot cover the final envelope.
  - I sign SHA-256 of `JSON.stringify({payloadType, payload: base64(statement without slot_lb_sig)})`. This is the "envelope" form that the 1.15 allow-list (`checkGateRecord`) already accepts for slot LB.
  - A verifier recomputes it by deleting `slot_lb_sig` and re-serializing. This works because the record is produced with `JSON.stringify` and `slot_lb_sig` is the gate's last key. A producer that serializes differently fails closed.
  - Please confirm, or define a tagged `tupleHash`.
- **DQ-1.12-2, `IDENTITY_POLICY_OK` cannot fail alone.** The seal forces each `idMethod` to be allowed by the policy, and `verifyAttestation` refuses a method other than `idMethod` (`METHOD_MISMATCH` or `METHOD_NOT_ALLOWED`). So any `IDENTITY_POLICY_OK` failure also fails `PASSPORT_x_OK`, as P1.12-07 already allows.
  - It compares the release method with the method of the *verified* onboarding attestation only. That keeps P1.12-05 ("only ONBOARDING_OK fails") and P1.12-07 both true.
  - `IDS_DISTINCT` and `IDENTITY_POLICY_OK` pass when there is nothing to compare (a record is missing or unreadable). The record's own code blocks the gate in that case.
- **DQ-1.12-3, P1.12-05's zero-knowledge row ("the identifier and the salt do not give C_side").** Under OD-2, the gate has no salt and does not recompute the commitment. That row is tested as "the attestation states another C_side" (`COMMITMENT_MISMATCH`).
- **DQ-1.12-4, the NFT URI.** It is read from the validated mint transaction, which is immutable. The current NFTokenPage is not read.
  - The NFTokenID's sequence field cannot be derived from the transaction, so only the issuer, taxon and flags are cross-checked against it.
