## Decisions
- Demo profile for the gate (mock proofs need devMode). Fresh proofs, verified live through the 3.11 adapter. Recorded loaders are used by 1.17's offline test, and the recorded sets by P2.9-01.
- `zkChain.ts` copies `openChain` and adds `idle()`: during a 20 s proof the ledger closer is stopped, otherwise the standalone server forgets the mint transaction (256 ledgers) before the gate reads it (I-2.9-b).
- Release queries have the face match off (headless prover limit); onboarding `expiry` uses CancelAfter + 30 days as in 1.17.
