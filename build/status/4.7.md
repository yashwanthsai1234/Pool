## Decisions and evidence
- Enrollment signing is async (WebCrypto), core's `EnrollmentSigner` is sync: core gets a placeholder signer, and each message is signed for real before sending; the sent copy is what transcripts and records use.
- Remote share 2 speaks first on the relay (`sign.begin` from the second device or HSM) because the vault opens share 1 only after the remote commits.
- `apiRelay` puts the signing pair first in `participants` (3.9's relay asks the first two); the contract has no `pair` field.
- Refresh keeps the old ciphertexts until every deletion statement is in, and restores them if any holder aborts. Found by a flaky test: without it, one holder could keep a new share after another aborted (signing then fails).
- xrpl stays out of the route chunks: screens import only `text.ts` and types, and load `recovery`/`rotation` logic through `import()`. `apps/web/vite.config.ts` stubs `@xrplf/mpt-crypto/wasm` (confidential MPT, unused) so no WASM is bundled.

## Design questions
- DQ-4.7-a: the DKG, refresh and repair run in the app page in these tests. In production they belong in the vault frame (4.10), with the relay bridged over `postMessage` (frame CSP is `connect-src 'none'`). Not built here.
- DQ-4.7-b: with a security key, the phone runs holders 1 and 2 (a FIDO2 key cannot run FROST); holder 2's enrollment key lives on the phone. Recovery therefore needs new keys for both, pinned through the recovery.
- DQ-4.7-c: a deletion statement is signed after the new share is stored, but the old ciphertext is dropped only at COMPLETE (ZF guidance).
