## Design
- Transfer: `LGXC`, version, tagged fields (DG1, SOD, DG14, DG15, AA answer), ascending, 1 MiB cap per field.
- QR text: `legxchip1:<session hex>:<challenge hex>:<X-Wing public key base64url>`. Challenge = `aaChallenge(presenceMsg)` (first 8 bytes, OD-23).
- Encryption: `PACKAGE_SUITES['hpke-xwing-v1']` from `@legx/core/pq` with info `legx/chip-link/v1` and aad = session id || challenge. I did not add a PackageKind (core is not mine), so I call the suite directly.
- Receiver: opens once (secret key zeroed), decodes, calls `chipChecks` (1.9). A failed open does not burn the session.
