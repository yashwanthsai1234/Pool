## Design
- Plain Fastify routes with `addContentTypeParser('application/json', {parseAs:'buffer'})`; auth (OD-5) runs on the raw bytes, then the oRPC procedure runs through `@orpc/server` `call`, which validates input and output with the contract schemas. Unknown fields give 400 with the field path.
- State: one `deal.state` column, changed only through `machine.transition` inside the evidence head lock, in the same transaction as the event and the outbox row (OD-9 payload).
- Public data and the seal are derived from the evidence log (key.bound, frost.dkg.completed, identity.onboarded) and the enrollment keys.
- The coordinator re-checks every ledger fact (validated tx, type, result), re-runs `checkLock` and the `GatePort` itself.
- Signature blobs are keyed (request, role); the combined blob is identical across retries; after a crash `recoverDeal` or a duplicate fourth blob finishes the request; `ledger.tx(hash)` is read before any re-submit.
