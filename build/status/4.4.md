## Decisions
- `SNAPSHOT_MAX_LAG = 5` ledgers (about 20 s): the snapshot must be that close to the public node's latest validated ledger, rechecked at click.
- Chip items after a failing check show `pending` ("not reached"), because `chipChecks` stops at the first failure. Zero-knowledge items are each computed, so all show PASS or FAIL.
- "Run lock check" is the gate's `LOCK_OK` line (one button runs the lock check and the gate on one snapshot).
- A sealed deal cannot name a method its `identityPolicy` forbids (`dealSeal` throws), so the console takes the method from the seal; a stored attestation of another method is the gate's `IDENTITY_POLICY_OK` finding (tested).
- New tag for the OD-19 list: `legx/hpke/release-code-handover/v1` (HPKE info and, inside `tupleHash`, the AAD with the deal id).
