# 4.6-ios-e2e Chip relay route, console receive, iOS QR scan

**State:** done. No real passport or physical phone tried; camera scan untested (simulator has no camera).

## Design
- `apps/server/src/chipLink.ts`: console `PUT /chip-link/:sid` opens a session (TTL 5 min, max 256); phone `POST /chip-link/:sid` `{sessionId, enc, ct}` (base64url) stores ONCE (replay 409, unopened/expired 404, mismatch/bad body 400); console `GET` polls (204 not yet, 200 returns and deletes). Server never decrypts.
- compose.ts: one registration line, plus a one-line `onRequest` exemption for `/chip-link/` (phone has no signing key; the QR's one-time key is the secret). Live feed is deal-authenticated and per deal, so a poll route was used.
- Console: `-console/chipRelay.ts` client; `ui.tsx` PassportCard gets "Receive from phone" (opens session, polls, `passport.check({kind:'reader', sealed})` -> `receiveChip` -> chipChecks).
- iOS: `QRScanner.swift` (AVCaptureMetadataOutput, .qr, ignores non-legxchip1), `Relay.swift` (URL `<base>/chip-link/<sid>`), `NSCameraUsageDescription`.
