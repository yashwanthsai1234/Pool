## Research notes
- **The C2SP 0x06 context [CHECK] is resolved.** The spec names none. All three implementations call ML-DSA with the empty context: Go torchwood (`crypto/mldsa`, `Sign(nil, m, crypto.Hash(0))`), transparency-dev/formats (`filippo.io/mldsa`) and cloudflare/azul (RustCrypto `signature::Verifier`). Evidence in both directions:
  1. A checkpoint made by the Go reference code (transparency-dev/formats; vendored from hupe1980/doubleentry at 2d76ec5) verifies in our verifier, both lines.
  2. `@cloudflare/signed-note-wasm` 0.3.0 (`MlDsa44NoteVerifier`, which is azul `tlog_cosignature::SubtreeV1NoteVerifier`) verifies our 0x06 line and our committed golden note. It rejects a line signed under `legx/checkpoint/v1`.

  So the `it.todo` asked by P1.16-13 is replaced by these passing tests. Sources: https://github.com/FiloSottile/torchwood/blob/main/cosignature.go, https://github.com/transparency-dev/formats/blob/main/note/note_cosigv1.go, https://github.com/cloudflare/azul/blob/main/crates/tlog_cosignature/src/subtree_v1.rs.
- **The signature-line marker is an em dash** (U+2014) plus a space, not `--` as IMPLEMENTATION P1.16-05 writes it. Source: https://c2sp.org/signed-note.
- **`CosignedSubtree`** uses RFC 9846 `opaque<1..2^8-1>` (a 1-byte length) for the name and the origin, and u64 big-endian numbers. Source: https://c2sp.org/tlog-cosignature@v1.1.0.
- **ACVP vectors** come from usnistgov/ACVP-Server at 975de31: 15 ML-DSA keyGen, 45 sigVer (with contexts and external mu), 10 deterministic sigGen and 40 ML-KEM-768 cases. noble 0.7.1 passes them all.
- **X-Wing draft-11 Appendix C:** noble `ml_kem768_x25519` and `@hpke/hybridkem-x-wing` 0.7.0 both reproduce all 3 vectors (keys, ct, ss), and `kem.id === 0x647a`. A 32-byte share seals to 1,168 bytes. Source: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-connolly-cfrg-xwing-kem/.
- **Node 24.22:** the seed exported with `raw-seed` gives the same public key in noble. Node's default context is empty, and the test asserts that a missing option fails.

  Please confirm them, or name the ones you want.
- **DQ-3:** I made some stricter choices than the text requires:
  - `sig_algs` must equal `["ed25519","ml-dsa-65"]` exactly, in that order;
  - signatures must be in the order Ed25519 then ML-DSA (as contracts `HybridSignatures` has it);
  - a binding carries exactly 2 holder signatures (3 is refused as `TOO_MANY_HOLDERS`);
  - `KEY_EXPIRED` also covers a time before `validFrom`;
  - checkpoints with extension lines are refused (0x06 does not sign them).
- **DQ-4:** The [CHECK] on the 0x06 context in DESIGN 6.13 can be removed (see Research notes).
