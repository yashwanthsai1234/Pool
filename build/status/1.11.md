## What changed in `packages/core/src/lockcheck.ts`
- **`Expected`.** `domainHex`, `signerPubKeys` and `quorum` are replaced by `deal: PublicDealData`, the sealed data. The checker reads the four signer keys, `lawyerOpsA`/`B`, the sealed `releaserAccount` and the Domain inputs from it. `releaserAccount` (the account expected to submit the finish), `credentialSubjects`, `escrow` and `allowedTicketSequences` stay. This follows DESIGN 6.9: "addresses derived from the four sealed public keys", "recomputed locally", "Expected holds the sealed lawyerOpsA, lawyerOpsB and releaserAccount". It also means a caller can no longer hand in signer keys that differ from the seal (there is a test for this).
- **`DOMAIN_IS_SEAL`.** Domain must be exactly 130 hex characters and equal `domain(contractRoot, dealSeal(deal))`. If `dealSeal` or the decoding throws, the rule fails; the checker does not throw.
- **`RELEASER_IS_OPS_B`.** The rule passes when `exp.releaserAccount === deal.lawyerOpsB && deal.releaserAccount === deal.lawyerOpsB`, and it is always emitted. `PREAUTH_ONLY_RELEASER` compares against the sealed `deal.releaserAccount`.
- **`QUORUM_MATCH`.** The quorum must equal 4, as the 6.9 rule says.
- **DQ-11 fix.** `Oracle` now reads its owner from `Owner`.
- **Two robustness fixes found by the new tests.** These are not behavior changes for well-formed rippled data:
  - `NO_FINISH_DELAY_TRAP` and `CANCEL_AFTER_MARGIN` now apply to every escrow that matches owner, amount and condition, not to the first one found. Before, a second matching escrow with a `FinishAfter` trap passed or failed depending on the order of `objects` (P1.11-07). The new rule is stricter, so it fails closed.
  - `<TYPE>` in the dynamic codes is mapped to `[A-Z0-9_]{1,64}`, and `detail` is capped at 1024 characters, so every finding validates against the frozen contracts `Finding` schema. Before, a 32-entry signer list gave a `detail` of about 1,200 characters.
- **Contracts type.** `Finding` is now the contracts type, re-exported.
- Removed `src/lockcheck.ts` and `test/lockcheck.test.ts` from the exclusion list in `packages/core/biome.json`.

## Known gaps
- **Wallet address.** The checker does not compare `acct.Account` with `deal.walletAddress`. DESIGN 6.9 has no such rule. The caller reads the wallet it means to check, and the Domain seal already binds `walletAddress`.
- **Malformed `SignerList`.** A `SignerList` without `SignerEntries` makes the checker throw, as the starting code did. rippled always returns that field. A throw is fail-closed for callers (no "pass" is produced).
- **`minCancelAfter`.** It is still computed by the caller (`close_time + 86400`), as in the starting code. The checker does not take `close_time` itself.
