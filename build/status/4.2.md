## What was built
- **`vault.ts`, `createVault(deps)`.** Bound to one party role. Functions:
  - `storeShares`, `storeSecondDeviceShare`;
  - `signFrost(dealId, SignRequest)` and `signMultisig`;
  - `cosigner()`, the second device's side;
  - `backupShare2ToFile`, `restore`, `installShare1` (the seam for 4.7's repair), `withIdentitySalt`, `list`, `location`.
- **`signFrost`:**
  - derives the bytes from the request itself;
  - checks them with 1.15 `messageAllowed` against the deal from the `deals` dependency;
  - unlocks share 2 (a PRF tap, or the code) before decrypting anything;
  - runs two `SigningSession`s (nonces used once and zeroed by 1.15), then `aggregateChecked` (`verifyShare`, aggregate, strict verify);
  - zeroes every buffer in `finally`.
- **Remote share 2 (second device or firm HSM):** goes through `CoSignerPort`. Share 1 is decrypted only after the remote holder commits.
- **Share-2 modes:** `security-key`, `single-device`, `recovery-code`, `second-device`, and `firm-hsm` (lawyers).
- **The identity salt** is stored with share 2, under the same lock, or on the second device.
- **`aead.ts`:** AES-GCM with an AAD of `legx/vault/v1|id|field`. HKDF from the PRF output, with the salt `tupleHash("legx/vault-prf-salt/v1",[dealId, slot])`. PBKDF2 at 600,000 iterations for the code.
- **`prf.ts`:** `webauthnPrf` over @simplewebauthn/browser 14.0.0. It keeps the credential's transports (see evidence).
- **`protocol.ts`:** the fixed zod schema and the MessagePort RPC (`serveVault`/`connectVault`, `serveCoSigner`/`connectCoSigner`). Errors cross the boundary as codes only. Storing shares and the recovery code are never on the wire.
- **`csp.ts`, `enrollment.ts`, `rules.ts`, `file.ts`, `recoveryCode.ts`, `store.ts`, `errors.ts`, `index.ts`.**
- **Seams:**
  - 4.7 runs DKG, repair and refresh inside the frame with `storeShares`, `installShare1`, `assertShareRecipient`, `holderRoles` and `enrollmentKeys().sign`. `refreshShares` is not here.
  - 4.10 hands a MessagePort to the cross-origin iframe after an `event.origin` check, serves `VAULT_CSP` as a header with `frame-ancestors`, and adds the hash self-check.
  - 4.9 plugs ML-DSA-65 in through `PqHalf`.

## Research notes
All URLs are in `packages/vault/SOURCES.md`.
- **CDP WebAuthn domain (pdl):** `VirtualAuthenticatorOptions` has `protocol`, `ctap2Version`, `transport`, `hasResidentKey`, `hasUserVerification`, `hasPrf`, `isUserVerified` and `automaticPresenceSimulation` (default true). Commands used: `getCredentials`, `setUserVerified`, `removeVirtualAuthenticator`. https://chromedevtools.github.io/devtools-protocol/tot/WebAuthn/ , https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ChromeDevTools/devtools-protocol/master/pdl/domains/WebAuthn.pdl
- **Vitest 5:** `cdp()` from `vitest/browser`. The provider opens it with `page.context().newCDPSession(page)` (read in node_modules), and it reaches the test iframe.
- **SimpleWebAuthn 14.0.0 (source):** `extensions` are spread into the options unchanged and `getClientExtensionResults()` comes back raw, so `prf.eval.first` can be a `Uint8Array` and `results.first` is an ArrayBuffer. https://github.com/MasterKale/SimpleWebAuthn/blob/master/packages/browser/src/methods/startAuthentication.ts
- **PRF:** `create` reports `prf.enabled`; `get` returns `results.first`. https://w3c.github.io/webauthn/#prf-extension , MDN WebAuthn extensions.

## Design questions
- **DQ-4.2-a (moving share 1 between iOS contexts, P4.2-13).**
  - The recovery file moves share 2 and the salt only. If it also carried share 1, file plus code (or file plus security key) would be a full key without the phone.
  - So share 1 in the new context comes from a repair (4.7, `installShare1`), and `restore` returns `needsShare1: true`.
  - Decide whether that is acceptable, or whether a PRF-locked share-1 move file is wanted.
- **DQ-4.2-b (recovery-code mode):** with no PRF, share 2 sits on the phone under a 128-bit code that the client keeps off the phone and types for each signature. I recorded the roles as `phone,second-authenticator,recovery-agent`, not single-device. Confirm, or label it single-device (IMPLEMENTATION table line 201 suggests so when the key is missing entirely).
- **DQ-4.2-c (where the seal comes from):** `signFrost` checks against `deps.deals(dealId)`, which the frame host supplies. It should be the frame's own verified copy (4.3/4.10), not data from the app page. The second device uses its own copy, never the phone's.
- **Role names** follow OD-17 (`second-authenticator`, `passkey-same-device` for single-device, as in 1.15).
