## Design (summary)
`WatcherService` wraps `WatcherClient` (2.7). Handler = `recordObservation`: one DB transaction with observation (on conflict nothing), outbox row `ledger.observation` (only if the row was new), cursor advance to `ledger - 1` (backfill only; the client moves it to the ledger after the whole ledger), then `pg_notify('legx_outbox', topic)` after commit. A stream message is filed under a watched party of the transaction. Ledger events from the primary (through a tap on the link) push `ledger.validated`, re-check agreement while paused, and lock-check every funded deal at that ledger index. Alerts and lock failures go to the outbox topic `alert`; evidence goes to an injected sink.

## Known gaps
- The evidence sink and `deals()` are ports; the real wiring (3.4 signing, deal registry) is not done here.
- One watcher process assumed (no advisory lock). Failover only moves forward (no automatic return to the first server). Disagreement is judged by the 2.7 client on the newest common ledger; Clio was not exercised, only a model second server.
- The lock check needs the server to still have the ledger at `index` (full history or recent).
