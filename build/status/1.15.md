## What the module does
- **`frost.ts`**:
  - typed `FrostError` (`code`, plus `culprit` = holder index);
  - point and scalar parsing that refuses wrong lengths, non-canonical encodings, the identity, small-order points, zero and values of L or more;
  - `slotPolicy`, `canonicalRoles` and `isSingleDeviceMode`; `slotPubkey` (0xED || key) and `slotAddress`;
  - `SigningSession`: private nonces, used once and zeroed. It returns `SESSION_MISMATCH` for another message or another commitment list, `NONCE_USED` for a second share, and `MESSAGE_REFUSED` when the signer's own `checkMessage(msg, list)` refuses, without using the nonce. `toJSON` throws, and there is no constructor or deserializer.
  - `verifyShares`, then `aggregateChecked`: it checks every share and names the cheater, then aggregates, then verifies strictly through 1.4's `verifyXrplKeySig`;
  - `canSign`; enrollment-signed messages (`enrollmentSigned`, `openEnrollmentMessage`), whose signed bytes contain the session id, kind, sender and recipient.
- **`frostDkg.ts`** has pure round functions:
  - every holder must be pinned (`PinCheck`) before round 1;
  - round 1 carries the commitments, the proof of knowledge (we check it ourselves, so a bad proof names its holder) and a per-session X-Wing KEM key;
  - the echo: each holder signs `transcriptHash`, and any difference aborts with `TRANSCRIPT_MISMATCH`;
  - round-2 shares are X-Wing HPKE ciphertexts, with the session and both parties in the AAD;
  - each share gets a Feldman check, and a bad share produces a signed complaint. `judgeComplaint` lets anyone name the cheater or the false accuser;
  - test-signing is bound to each pair (`testSignMessage(ctx, pub, pair)`). `testSignCheck` signs only its own pair's message with exactly that pair's commitments;
  - `dkgCompletedRecord` returns `SLOT_SETUP_FAILED` unless 3 echoes cover one transcript, the public data follows from round 1, and all 3 pair signatures verify.
- **`frostRefresh.ts`**:
  - zero-constant dealings; a threshold change or a non-zero constant term is refused with `REFRESH_INVALID`; every message is enrollment-authenticated;
  - each sub-share gets a Feldman check, and a failure names the dealer with `SUBSHARE_INVALID` and replaces no share;
  - `ROTATION_REQUIRED` for a slot flagged stolen; `deletionStatement`; `refreshedEvent` is COMPLETE only when every remaining holder signed one with its pinned key over the exact old and new verifying shares;
  - the module comment says that a refresh is hygiene, not revocation.
- **`frostRepair.ts`**: ZF `repairable` (Laing and Stinson) on noble. Each helper also publishes commitments to its deltas, so the new holder can name a cheating helper. A share is installed only if it matches the participant's verifying share.
- **`frostSetup.ts`**:
  - `EnrollmentRegistry`: append-only, with `KEY_CHANGED`, `REGISTRY_APPEND_ONLY`, `KEY_NOT_PINNED` and `KEY_NOT_LOGGED`; its `.check` is the DKG `PinCheck`;
  - QR pinning with a 6-digit SAS;
  - `verifyServiceKeyList`: the anchored hash plus the operations-account signature. Ed25519 and secp256k1 keys both work, through `verifyXrplKeySig`.
- **`frostRules.ts`**:
  - `approvalCheck`: a WebAuthn L3 assertion (ES256), returning `APPROVAL_MISSING`, `APPROVAL_NOT_UV`, `APPROVAL_HASH_MISMATCH` or `APPROVAL_SIG_INVALID`;
  - `messageAllowed`:
    - XRPL multisign is allowed only for this wallet, and only for a Payment of the exact amount to sealed `payoutA` or `payoutB` (account plus tag), or an AccountDelete to `payoutA`;
    - it refuses: other fields, partial payment and other flags, a fee above `maxFeeDrops`, non-uint32 integer fields, and bytes that are not the transaction's `encodeForMultiSigning`;
    - five tagged tupleHash messages are allowed, each with the parts that must match the seal and the slot;
    - a gate record must be the SHA-256 of a `release.gate.passed` envelope for this deal, and is allowed for slot LB only;
  - **`cosign`**: the allow-list plus a user-verified approval for `firm-hsm` and `second-authenticator`. It is meant to be passed as `checkMessage`;
  - `commitmentId`, `NonceLedger` and `MemoryNonceLedger`, `releaseShare`;
  - the recovery machine (`recoveryStart`, `recoveryCancel`, `recoveryAuthorize`; the 72 h delay; notices for the old device and both lawyers; one record per step);
  - `rotationPlan`.

## Research notes
- noble `@noble/curves` 2.4.0 `abstract/frost.js` (read in node_modules):
  - `DKG.round3` runs a Feldman check on each received share, but without naming the sender, so we check first;
  - `signShare` zeroes the nonces;
  - `aggregate` already runs a cofactored verify; strict mode is our addition;
  - `ed25519_FROST` is a frozen object;
  - `randomScalar` maps 48 rng bytes to `(LE mod (n-1)) + 1`, which is how the vector tests inject scalars.
- RFC 9591 (sections 5.1, 5.2, 7.3, 7.7): signers must validate the message and never reuse nonces. https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9591
- ZF frost-core 3.0.0:
  - `dkg.rs`: the PoK challenge is `H_dkg(id || phi || R)`;
  - `refresh.rs`: the threshold is fixed, part 2 skips the PoK, and the caller authenticates senders;
  - `repairable.rs`: deltas, sigma, sum;
  - in `vectors_dkg.json`, `signing_shares[j]` is the share *received* from j.
  - https://github.com/ZcashFoundation/frost/tree/frost-ed25519/v3.0.0
- ZF docs: DKG needs a real broadcast channel, and round 2 must be authenticated and confidential. Old shares should be deleted only after everyone has refreshed. https://frost.zfnd.org/tutorial/dkg.html, https://frost.zfnd.org/tutorial/refreshing-shares.html
- FROST20 https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/852; Feldman VSS https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.1987.4; GJKR complaints https://doi.org/10.1007/s00145-006-0347-3; Laing and Stinson https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/1155; WebAuthn L3 https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/#sctn-verifying-assertion. Every URL is also listed in `packages/core/SOURCES.md`.

## Design questions
- **DQ-1.15-a, role strings.**
  - DESIGN 6 (encoding rules) and the py-ref golden vectors use `second-authenticator`. IMPLEMENTATION P1.15-09 says `security-key`, and `@legx/contracts` `HolderRole` has `security-key` and `second-device` but no `second-authenticator`.
  - I followed DESIGN; `slotPolicy` matches `tools/py-ref/vectors/slot_policy.json`. Single-device mode uses `phone,passkey-same-device,recovery-agent`.
- **DQ-1.15-b, complaint evidence.** DESIGN rule 3 says "a signed complaint with the ciphertext, so everyone can see who cheated".
  - A decrypted share alone cannot be tied to the ciphertext.
  - So each holder publishes a fresh per-session X-Wing key in its signed round-1 message (it is inside the echoed transcript), and a complaint reveals that session secret. Anyone can then decrypt the accused's signed ciphertext and run the Feldman check.
  - The revealed key only opens shares of the aborted session.
- **DQ-1.15-c, gate records.** DESIGN 6.4 lists gate records among the "tagged tupleHash" messages, but 6.17 has the slot sign the record's hash, which is the SHA-256 of the envelope. I implemented the 6.17 form.
- **DQ-1.15-d, freshness and approvals.**
  - DESIGN says "fresh" for the recovery proof without a number. I used `PROOF_FRESH_MS` = 10 min, with `PROOF_STALE` as the code.
  - A cancel after the delay is refused with `CANCEL_TOO_LATE`; a cancel with a bad signature with `CANCEL_SIG_INVALID`.
  - `second-authenticator` needs a UV approval just as `firm-hsm` does.
- **DQ-1.15-e, rotation transactions.** Rotation (P1.15-20, task 2.6) will need SignerListSet and AccountSet `Domain` on the allow-list. They are not allowed yet; a rotation context should add them.
- New internal tags, not in DESIGN or py-ref:
  - `legx/frost-msg/v1`, `frost-dkg-transcript/v1`, `frost-dkg-r2/v1`, `frost-test-sign/v1` (the pair is inside), `frost-refresh/v1`;
  - `frost-commitments/v1`, `frost-commitment-id/v1`, `frost-verifying-shares/v1`;
  - `enrollment-key/v1`, `pin-sas/v1`, `service-key-list/v1`, `recovery-cancel/v1`.

## Known gaps
- **P1.15-13 (ZF Rust cross-test, both directions) is not done.** There is no Rust toolchain, by rule.
  - The committed ZF vector files are the A/B test from the Rust side.
  - "Rust verifies our signature" is not run. Our signatures pass strict RFC 8032 verification, which is what ZF's Ed25519 verify checks.
  - T-U-23c compares with a transliteration of ZF `refresh_dkg_shares`, not a ZF run, because ZF ships no refresh vectors.
- Enrollment signatures are Ed25519 only until 1.16 lands, so they are not hybrid yet.
- P1.15-22 (budget) is timed by wall clock and takes the best of 3 runs, so a loaded machine does not fail it. Under coverage and parallel agents a single run went over 250 ms.
- T-P-01 builds its slots with noble's DKG without transport, for speed. Transport, echo and complaints are covered in `frost.dkg.test.ts`. The property still takes 25 to 85 s.
- `frost.dkg.completed` holds `testSignatures` per pair, but a signature alone cannot prove which shares made it. The proof comes from each holder's `testSignCheck`, which signs only its own pair's message with that pair's commitments.
- Biome reports 301 style warnings (`!`) in the test files; it reports no errors.
